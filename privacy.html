Privacy notice

Supply Chain Resilience Check · Last updated 5 October 2026

This notice explains what happens to your information when you use the Blaiklock Supply Chain Resilience Check, and the rights you have over it. It adds to our main website privacy policy, which explains how we handle personal information more generally.

Who we are

The check is run by Blaiklock Limited ("Blaiklock", "we", "us"), a company registered in England and Wales under company number 05161195. Our registered office is c/o TaxAssist Accountants, 635 Bath Road, Burnham, Slough, Berkshire, SL1 6AE, United Kingdom.

We are the controller of the personal information described in this notice. For any privacy question or request, email us at info@blaiklock.uk.com.

If you only take the check

  • Your answers are processed in your own browser to calculate your score and advice. They are not sent to us.
  • So that a page refresh doesn't lose your progress, your answers are kept in your browser's session storage. This is cleared when you close the tab. It is not a cookie and isn't used for anything else.
  • We don't use cookies, analytics or any tracking on this page, and the page's fonts and images are served from the same website, so no data is sent to third parties just by visiting it.

If you ask for a deeper review

If you choose "Yes" to a deeper review and submit your details, we receive:

  • your name, company name and email address, and your phone number if you give it
  • the area you'd most like us to look at, and where your supply chain operates
  • your answers to the 10 questions and your scores
  • whether you've signed up to our monthly newsletter

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To respond to your request for a review of your results and to discuss how we could help. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries, and taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To send you our monthly newsletter, only if you tick the optional box. Our lawful basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any newsletter or by emailing us.

We don't use your information for automated decision-making, and we never sell it.

Who we share it with

  • Formspree, Inc., based in the USA, which receives the form and forwards it to us by email. Formspree acts as our data processor under a data processing agreement, which requires it to protect your information, including when it is transferred outside the UK.
  • Microsoft, which provides our email system (Microsoft 365), where your enquiry is stored once it reaches us.
  • Mailchimp (The Rocket Science Group LLC, an Intuit company, based in the USA), which we use to send our monthly newsletter. We only add you to Mailchimp if you tick the newsletter box. Mailchimp acts as our data processor under a data processing agreement, which requires it to protect your information, including when it is transferred outside the UK.

We don't share your information with anyone else, including other companies or offices, unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

  • We keep review requests for 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete them. If you become a customer, our main privacy policy applies instead.
  • We delete submissions from Formspree within 30 days of them reaching our inbox.
  • If you've signed up to our newsletter, we keep your name and email address on our Mailchimp mailing list until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you
  • correct information that's wrong or incomplete
  • delete your information
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • transfer it to another organisation
  • stop sending you our newsletter at any time

Email info@blaiklock.uk.com to use any of these rights. We'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Changes to this notice

We'll update this page if anything changes and show the date of the latest version at the top.
