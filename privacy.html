If you only take the check

  • Your answers are processed in your own browser to calculate your score and advice. They are not sent to us.

If you ask for a deeper review

If you choose "Yes" to a deeper review and submit your details, we receive:

  • your name, company name and email address, and your phone number if you give it
  • the area you'd most like us to look at, and where your supply chain operates
  • your answers to the 10 questions and your scores
  • whether you've chosen to receive marketing emails

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To respond to your request for a review of your results and to discuss how we could help. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries, and taking steps at your request before any contract.
  • To send you occasional news and insights, only if you tick the optional box. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us.

We don't use your information for automated decision-making, and we never sell it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you
  • correct information that's wrong or incomplete
  • delete your information
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • transfer it to another organisation
  • stop sending you marketing at any time

Email info@blaiklock.uk.com to use any of these rights. We'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.
